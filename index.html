About

Built by someone who still sits with the blank page.

Ayin Studio started from a simple frustration: the tools professional teams rely on are rarely built for the way those teams actually work. They are either too generic, too heavy, or designed by people who have never had to live with the consequences of a bad interface at 11 p.m.

Most of the time that means sitting with how a team already works — the systems they design around, the data they depend on, the questions research keeps surfacing — and building something that fits that reality, not a template of someone else’s.

Ayin is the Hebrew letter for “eye,” and in older traditions it also points to the fertile nothing from which something new can appear. That is the work: starting from zero, seeing clearly, and making the tool that was missing.

What I care about

  • 01

    Tools that get used

    Software that sits unused is expensive theatre. I design for the daily workflow, not the pitch deck.

  • 02

    Clarity over cleverness

    Complex problems deserve simple interfaces. The intelligence should live in the system, not in the user’s head.

  • 03

    Starting from nothing

    Every engagement begins with a blank page and a real problem. No templates pretending to be custom.

  • 04

    Stay close to the problem

    If it is hard to explain in plain language, the software is not ready yet. Clarity is part of the build.

How we work

The discovery sprint

We usually begin with a short conversation and a shared map of the problem. The aim is not a long plan — it is a small first version the team can already use and react to.

01

Listen

A short call. No deck. We talk about the problem, what is getting in the way, and what would feel useful if it worked next month.

02

Map

Together we sketch the workflows, the data, and the decisions that matter. Enough structure to build from — not a long requirements document.

03

Build the first cut

A working piece that already helps with part of the problem. Something the team can try, not only review.

04

Refine & hand over

We adjust based on how the team actually uses it, then leave notes and ownership so it can keep growing without us.
